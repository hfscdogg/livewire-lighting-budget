LIVEWIRE LIGHTING AND SHADING ESTIMATOR

STAFF WORKFLOW
Open the approved company estimator link in the browser on a computer or iPad.
Sign in with an approved Google work account. All staff use the same hosted
application, shared project database and company pricing releases.

Start a project and add rooms. Select the lighting, controls, keypads, fixtures,
fans and shades involved. Record the surface, permission for access cuts,
attic access, working height and occupancy. Common technical settings are
inferred from these answers and remain available for adjustment.

Review the Budget screen's range, generated scope and installation assumptions.
Required facts appear in their relevant workflow step. Unchecked D2/D2R ceiling
suitability appears as an estimator follow-up before ordering. Known physical
incompatibility, blocked wiring or unsupported scope still requires resolution.

SHARED PROJECTS
Choose Save project to store the job, room notes and photos in the company
database. Choose Shared projects to find and reopen an existing job. Save before
starting the next job, signing out or leaving the page. Unsaved changes are not
available to teammates. If another employee changes the same job, reopen the
newer saved revision before continuing; conflicting saves are not overwritten.

CUSTOMER PDF
After the required budget review, choose Customer budget / PDF and Save / print
PDF in the browser. The document includes the planning range, room work,
installation methods, shared work and exclusions. The retainer is 4% of the
displayed low budget, credited toward the project, not added to its total.
Supplier prices, internal labor data, private notes and photos are excluded.
A pending estimate produces a scope summary without customer dollar amounts.

ESTIMATOR HANDOFF PDF
Choose Estimator handoff PDF for the internal report. It includes the range,
retainer, room selections, access, materials, labor, ordering notes and room
photos/notes. Save the shared project so estimating can reopen the editable job.
Keep the handoff internal and send the separate customer PDF to the client.

PRICING AND REVIEW
The repository's pricing/catalog.json is the maintained pricing source.
Saved projects retain their assigned catalog release until deliberately
repriced. Sales staff can view company pricing; pricing administrators and
owners maintain releases and approve customer budgets. Staff do not maintain
individual price lists or import spreadsheets as part of normal estimating.

Labor uses $112.50 per person-hour. Completed task hours round up to quarter-
hours after quantity and applicable condition adjustments. Programming includes
one startup hour plus per-device entry, activation and response testing.
The customer budget is -10% to +15% around the complete planning amount.
Drywall/plaster repair and painting are excluded. See LABOR-REVIEW.md and
SCOPE-MATRIX.txt for estimating allowances, inclusions and limits.

APPLICATION SUPPORT
The company Vercel deployment serves the application. Google verifies approved
employee accounts. PostgreSQL stores shared projects, pricing releases, notes
and photos. GitHub stores code and catalog source, not saved customer jobs.
See VERCEL-DEPLOYMENT.md for environment configuration and acceptance checks.
Sign-in, shared saving, photo handling, PDF output and backup restoration must
be accepted in the company environment before staff rollout.
