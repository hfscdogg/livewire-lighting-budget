# Guided room flow review

Reviewed November 16, 2026.

The sequence presents one customer decision at a time: work to include, control experience, keypad design when applicable, and wall appearance. Common settings are inferred; advanced settings remain available from the room overview and Conditions. Manufacturer product photos appear only in the keypad design question. They are unmodified style references, embedded for offline use, and do not select a finish or engraving beyond the stated budget basis.

## Verification

The guided-room-flow regression covers existing-light replacements, mixed scopes, hybrid dimmer substitution, companions, local versus centralized load wiring, reversible inferred platforms, preserved manual settings, saved/server input and occupied-room labor equivalence. The application event test follows both the keypad branch and its Back actions through the customer budget. Existing blocked-route and physical compatibility checks remain in the full regression suite. iPad Safari, camera interaction and visual PDF pagination are not verified by the DOM simulation.
