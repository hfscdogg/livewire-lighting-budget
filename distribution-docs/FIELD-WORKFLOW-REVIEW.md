# Field workflow and source integrity

Current workflow: R9, November 17, 2026.

## Guided decisions

Sales records project scope, customer preferences and observable site conditions. The estimator derives compatible lighting/control systems, assemblies, shared equipment and installation allowances. Room needs open the applicable detail section; the room overview is a read-only summary. Keypads, design and wall appearance are progressive choices. Existing-light controls have their own scope without requiring new fixtures.

The access sequence starts with Open framing, Drywall or Plaster, then applicable cut permission and attic access. Height and occupancy remain visible. Additional route, wiring and room settings are available as overrides. Later rooms can inherit visible assumptions; explicit condition reuse applies only to selected rooms. Fishing evidence, fan support, ceiling suitability, photos and notes are not copied as verified site facts.

Back, Change this choice, Undo and saving remain available when forward progression requires an answer. No-cut checks concern only new concealed routes required by the selected work. Existing-location replacement does not create an unrelated cable-route questionnaire. Connecting wiring follows the room access allowance unless an estimator override is recorded.

## Budget and handoff

The Budget screen presents the complete range, credited retainer and narrative scope. Actionable missing items lead to the appropriate input. Unchecked D2/D2R ceilings produce ordering follow-up rather than a missing-price warning; known incompatible conditions remain unresolved.

Customer output excludes supplier prices, internal labor, private notes and photos. The estimator handoff includes selected scope, conditions, quantities, task hours, notes and photos. Quarter-hour rounding is part of the calculation and is consistent between labor totals and reports.

## Input and package checks

Negative quantities and invalid prices remain errors rather than becoming free work. Duplicate room identifiers are rejected. Source verification checks each recorded file, including nested folders, against its release digest. Tests exercise missing folders and mixed-release uploads. The package includes a GitHub workflow for source verification, tests and builds.

Automated interaction checks simulate application events and inspect generated markup; they do not replace physical iPad/browser acceptance. Company acceptance should include touch navigation, camera photos, save/reopen and both PDF reports. Shared acceptance also includes employee sign-in, simultaneous edits and persistence after deployment.
