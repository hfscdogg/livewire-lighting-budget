LIVEWIRE SHADING BUDGET: SCOPE AND ASSUMPTIONS
Budget basis revision 10 | November 13, 2026 | USD and feet

WHAT IS PRICED
The current company catalog carries the UPPER historical size-band selling
amount as the assembly budget, then calculates the complete project range at
-10%/+15%. A matching job-specific quote takes precedence. The original low
and high values for all 36 bands are preserved. The customer document identifies
this historical basis. It is not a current fabrication quote or a guarantee.
Fabric/options must fit the allowance or be repriced before commitment.

The 36 low/high rows were recovered from Livewire's September 2026 shade
budget work: 12 size bands each for Triathlon, Sivoia and Palladiom. These are
historical CUSTOMER SELLING budget ranges, not dealer cost, MSRP or a current
fabrication quote. The app does not add another material margin. Approximate
width and height each round up to the next recovered band. There is no
square-foot extrapolation. The 9-by-9-foot grid boundary is NOT a manufacturer's
maximum shade size. Roller shades use this provisional grid; other styles and
out-of-grid sizes require a custom price and installation scope.

The exact fabric, power variant, tube/drive, hembar, brackets, batteries,
receiver, top treatment and complete package inclusions must be checked.
A final-measure/fabrication review is required for every order. An inside/outside
mount choice does not certify clearance or anchorage. Blackout fabric alone
does not remove light at the edges. Dual layers count two assemblies and drives.

HISTORICAL INFRASTRUCTURE ALLOWANCES
- $2,153 per started 10 combined NEW wired Sivoia/Palladiom shades, pooled by
  physical power location. This is a bundled selling allowance, not a selected
  supply/enclosure/module SKU or a guaranteed 10-drive electrical capacity.
- $200 per NEW wired Sivoia/Palladiom shade route. A provisional 75-foot medium
  route is used for crew/time planning. Existing compatible power avoids this
  new-power route allowance but still gets test/connection work.
- These packages have no recovered material/labor split. No split is invented.
  The taxable material percentage must be configured before a complete
  tax-inclusive range is released. Neither package receives another margin.
- To prevent double charging, base wiring and power-package installation labor
  is treated as included in the package price. Its hours still count toward
  crew days and travel. Only incremental condition-related hours are separately
  charged. This treatment is a declared budgeting assumption to be calibrated.
- Exact enclosure, installed power modules, supply feed/receptacle, output load,
  wire size, voltage drop and power-group separation need review. A checkbox
  records that review; the app does not perform an electrical design.

POWER AND CONTROL ARE DIFFERENT
Triathlon remains RF-controlled whether battery or wired power. Its 12 V
supply/cable is separate from 35 V Sivoia/Palladiom supplies. The historical
35 V package does NOT price a Triathlon supply. Battery operation adds no
wired power panel. Battery-to-wired field conversion must not be assumed.

RF shades contribute to shared Type-A planning counts, never Type-X. A shade
and its receiver are modeled as one endpoint, with the receiver separately
priced only if not in the assembly. Confirm model-specific Designer addressing.
RadioRA 3 currently limits usable ordinary Type-A devices to 95 per processor
and 190 per system; additional processors do not erase the system limit.
HomeWorks wired QS shade drives use a wired QS link, not a wireless-only
processor port. Counts, coverage, power and link limits still require design.

Palladiom Wired requires 35 V power and wired QS communication on HomeWorks.
Palladiom Wire-Free uses RF commands with a battery or compatible wired-power
configuration. A wired-power RF drive uses the specified receiver and adapter.
Check existing Sivoia power and communication separately. The historical
Palladiom grid covers a preliminary allowance only. Confirm the actual drive,
size and allowance basis before releasing a budget range; confirm fabrication
pricing before ordering.

A wired power route does not prove a QS communication route exists. New wired
QS shade packages provisionally include the shade-to-power-group control/power
route; a shared power-group-to-processor QS backbone is counted separately once
per location. Existing power can be reused while needing new control conductors.
Only verified existing control routes receive the corresponding reuse credit.
Shade supplies are separate from keypad PDU power. Lighting DPMs are not added
for shade drives. Lighting and shades share compatible headends and controls.

Standalone wireless shades default to one room-level Pico, not one per shade
and no lighting processor. System-integrated shades can reuse app/keypad scenes;
the tool does not automatically add one new keypad per window. Existing scene
capacity and coverage need review. Full scheduling/integration scope is not
unlimited or included automatically.

LABOR AND TRAVEL
The recovered 2 hours per window is interpreted as 2 PERSON-HOURS per complete
shade assembly, charged at the current $112.50/person-hour. Dual layers count
twice. This is a planning interpretation, not a claim about measured production.
Basic installation covers brackets, assembly mounting/leveling, activation,
pairing, limits and cycle test. Generic endpoint commissioning is not charged
again for the same shade. Off-site system grouping/programming is separate.

Additional preliminary standards:
- Final measure/survey: .35 person-hour per opening.
- Existing treatment removal: .35 per opening, when selected.
- New shade cable: .035 per foot of provisional route.
- Supply-package installation: 2 per package.
- Existing power verification/connection: .35 per drive.
- Each selected simple accessory fitting increment: .50 per assembly.
- Room Pico fitting/pairing: .35 per remote.
- Off-site system grouping: .20 per group.
These are estimating judgments, not published trade standards. Existing
condition factors apply only to affected physical/wiring tasks. No blanket
whole-job factor is added. Base package work is not billed twice.

A shade job defaults to two planned visits for survey and installation.
Travel is the greater of planned visits and required eight-hour days for one
two-person crew (16 on-site person-hours/day), with daily setup. Actual visit
scheduling remains a project decision. Shared lighting/shading setup and travel
are counted together, not as two independent projects.

UNRESOLVED OR EXCLUDED SCOPE
Historical assembly allowances remain available. Receiver/Pico/supply costs
and optional parts remain unpriced when no verified company value exists. Specialty shapes,
drapery/Roman/honeycomb/wood systems, oversized assemblies, coupled systems,
structural blocking, lift work, pockets, electrical service upgrades, hazardous
materials, finish repairs/painting, removal disposal and complex integrations
need their own supplier/site scope. Capturing a preference is not a complete
quote. The customer PDF remains Budget pending with no dollar total while
required dependencies are unresolved.
