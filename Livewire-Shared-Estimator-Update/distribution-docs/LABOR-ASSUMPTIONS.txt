LIVEWIRE LABOR ASSUMPTIONS - INSTALLATION PLANNING REVISION 1

Rate: $112.50 per person-hour. These are preliminary estimating allowances,
not measured Livewire production history or published labor-unit standards.

CALCULATION
Task hours = quantity x hours per unit x applicable condition factor.
Cable material and pulling labor use the same footage. Layout/pull preparation,
fishing setup, devices/terminations and surface removal are separate tasks.
The greatest applicable route/access factor applies to the complete cable run.
Factors are not multiplied together. Device work uses working access and applicable overhead working height,
not the cable-fishing factor. Commissioning and off-site programming remain
separate. One two-person crew supplies 16 person-hours per eight-hour workday.

INSTALLATION DECISIONS
New construction, remodel and retrofit select fixture installation tasks.
They do not infer accessible framing, permission to remove finishes or a clear
fishing path. Ceiling, wall and shared routes are recorded separately.

Open framing: cable factor 1.00.
Accessible attic, floor or cavity route: factor 1.25.
Verified concealed fishing: drywall 1.75, plaster/lath 2.25.
Selective removal: drywall 1.35, plaster/lath 1.60, plus access openings.
Continuous approved removal: factor 1.15, plus removal area.
Restricted working access: limited 1.25, difficult 1.60.
Working height over 10 to 12 ft on clear level flooring: physical task factor 1.25.
Higher, sloped or stairwell work requires a separately priced access plan.

For selective removal, each route's length is divided across its modeled
ceiling, wall and shared segments. The allowance is one opening per started
25 ft of closed segment per pull. This is a planning quantity, not a measured
penetration count. The room's estimator override replaces its selective-opening
count. A zero override does not remove the need to establish a no-opening route.
Continuous removal uses that closed-segment footage x a one-foot strip width.
Overlapping routes must be reviewed to avoid overstating removal. Selective
and continuous removal are alternatives for a segment, not cumulative charges.
Normal fixture/box cutouts are included in installation. New plaster cutouts
add only the careful-cutting increment. No repair, reinstatement or painting.

An unknown or blocked route with no allowed openings holds the customer price.
Documented fishing, permission for openings or an alternate priced method is
required. No attic alone neither proves nor rules out a fishable route.
Shared cable routes and shade-only work receive the same checks.
An existing fixture replacement needing no new cable creates no route charge.

Occupied rooms add contents protection and work-area isolation. Masonry,
fire-rated assemblies, structural changes, suspected hazardous material,
special fixture handling and exceptional working heights hold the automatic
budget for a separate reviewed method and price. The application does not
perform an electrical or structural design.

SCOPE BOUNDARIES
Fixture mounting/termination, removal of existing fixtures and aiming are
separate. New boxes are separate from device installation. Branch runs,
central load home runs, control drops, QS cable, low-voltage lighting leads,
Ethernet and shade cable each retain their applicable task and material.
Kit-included equipment still receives installation labor. Hybrid dimmers do
not duplicate a separate local dimmer. Shared headends are counted by system.
Basic programming allows up to four simple scenes per room. Specialized
integrations need a separate scope. Travel uses inferred workdays or the
minimum planned visits, whichever is greater. Rough/trim work defaults to two
visits. Additional workday setup is included.

Saved estimates retain their prices. Updated installation rules require review
before a customer range can be reused. Current company standards are maintained
in pricing/catalog.json and exported through the application's pricing tools.

CEILING FAN INSTALLATION
Existing fans can remain with controls only, or receive no new control.
Replacement includes 0.50 PH removal, 2.00 PH assembly/hanging/connection/
balancing/test, and 0.25 PH support verification per fan when suitable existing
support is confirmed. New or replacement support adds 1.00 PH and a fan-rated
box/brace. FBRS4200R uses the previously supplied $45 material-cost allowance;
confirm 16–24 inch wood framing and fan weight within its UL 70 lb rating.
New locations add the branch wiring route with normal access/removal logic.
Height and physical access affect installation tasks. Fan purchase is owner
supplied; separate light controls and specialty/heavy/sloped mounting require
explicit scope. New Lutron headends are not added solely for fans using their
existing controls or supplied remotes.
